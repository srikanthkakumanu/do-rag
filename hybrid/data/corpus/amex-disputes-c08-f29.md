# American Express Core Dispute Codes

Synthetic summary for demonstration. Not official American Express rule text.

## C08 Goods or services not received

Code C08 applies when the card member did not receive goods or services. The merchant has 20 days to respond to the inquiry or chargeback.

Remedies for C08:

- Proof of delivery signed by the card member.
- Evidence that services were rendered on the agreed date.

## F29 Card not present fraud

Code F29 applies when the card member denies participating in a card-not-present charge. The merchant has 20 days to respond.

Remedies for F29:

- Proof that the merchant used SafeKey authentication.
- Evidence that the goods were shipped to the card member billing address.
