# Mastercard 4837 No Cardholder Authorization

Synthetic summary for demonstration. Not official Mastercard rule text.

## Overview

Reason code 4837 applies when the cardholder claims they did not authorize or participate in the transaction.

## Response window

The merchant has 45 calendar days from the chargeback date to submit a second presentment.

## Remedies

- Proof of a successful 3-D Secure authentication for the transaction.
- Evidence of two or more prior undisputed transactions with the same device and shipping address.
- Proof that the issuer approved the transaction with a valid authorization and the card was present with chip read.

## Limits

An issuer may not use 4837 when the cardholder account has more than 35 fraud chargebacks in the same account within the program window.
