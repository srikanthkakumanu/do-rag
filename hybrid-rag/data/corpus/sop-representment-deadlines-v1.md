# SOP Internal Representment Deadlines

Internal operating procedure (synthetic example).

## Internal submission buffer

Analysts must submit representment packages 5 calendar days before the network response deadline.

## Escalation

Cases with less than 3 calendar days remaining are escalated to the disputes team lead.
