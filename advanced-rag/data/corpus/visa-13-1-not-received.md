# Visa 13.1 Merchandise or Services Not Received

Synthetic summary for demonstration. Not official Visa rule text.

## Overview

Reason code 13.1 applies when the cardholder paid but did not receive the merchandise or services by the agreed date.

## Cardholder waiting period

If no delivery date was agreed, the cardholder must wait 15 calendar days from the transaction date before initiating the dispute.

## Response window

The merchant must respond within 30 calendar days of the dispute processing date.

## Remedies

- Proof of delivery to the address provided by the cardholder, including a carrier tracking record.
- For digital goods, logs showing the cardholder downloaded or accessed the goods.
- Evidence that the cardholder cancelled the order before the delivery date is not a remedy.
