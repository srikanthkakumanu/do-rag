# Discover Fraud Dispute Codes

Synthetic summary for demonstration. Not official Discover rule text.

## UA01 Fraud card present

UA01 applies to fraudulent card-present transactions. Evidence of a chip read or a signed receipt is the primary remedy.

## UA02 Fraud card not present

UA02 applies when the cardholder denies a card-not-present transaction. The merchant has 30 calendar days to respond.

Remedies for UA02:

- ProtectBuy authentication results.
- Evidence of prior undisputed transactions from the same device.
