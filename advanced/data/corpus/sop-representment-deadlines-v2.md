# SOP Internal Representment Deadlines

Internal operating procedure (synthetic example).

## Internal submission buffer

Analysts must submit representment packages 7 calendar days before the network response deadline.

## Escalation

Cases with less than 4 calendar days remaining are escalated to the disputes team lead and the operations manager.
